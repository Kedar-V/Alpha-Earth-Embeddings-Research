Method

Per city × settlement (urban / suburban / rural): prefer correct cells with P(res)>0.9 / <0.1 (≥3 each). If a side is thin, widen to 0.7/0.3, then any correct, then GT extremes by P(res). Twelve equal steps between those centroids.

Patches: cosine NN in LORO PLS-DA dims 1–32 over that city×settlement pool (skip a repeat of the previous cell when a second neighbor exists).

Curve: same steps in LORO PLS dims 1–3, scored with that city's PLS-3 logistic (extract LORO fold). NN patches still use PLS-32.

Boundary walk

Per-city straight line from high-confidence correct residential centroid (P(res)>0.9) to non-residential centroid (P(res)<0.1) in LORO PLS space. Each frame is the nearest real 10 m patch (PLS-32 cosine NN); the curve is LORO PLS-3 logistic P(res) on the parallel PLS-3 centroid path.

Performance dashboard

Classification (accuracy, macro-F1, AUROC) and probability fit (Brier, MAE, score gap) across cities. DoU-balanced sample = equal cells per city, and within each city equal urban / suburban / rural with 1:1 res vs non-res; LORO holdout = official leave-one-city/metro-out metrics (not DoU-stratified).
