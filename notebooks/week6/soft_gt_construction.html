Week 6 · soft GT

How soft ground truth is created

Same Overture footprints as hard labels — but the target is mean soft cell coverage inside each building, not 0/1 y_res.

RES footprint NRES footprint AE 10 m cell inside_polygon cells
1

Overture footprints (EPSG:4326)

Track-A buildings with locked resi_def_v1. Hard GT would stop here as (1,0) or (0,1). Soft GT keeps going.

2

Overlay AlphaEarth 10 m cells (per-city UTM)

Each metro keeps its own AE CRS (e.g. Boston EPSG:32619, Denver EPSG:32613). Cells whose centers fall inside the footprint are tagged inside_polygon.

3

Per-cell soft fractions (week4 pixel GT)

Already computed once in build_week4_pixel_gt.py. For each AE cell, intersect RES vs NRES footprint unions and divide by cell area.

frac_res(c) = area( ∪ RES footprints ∩ c ) / area(c)
frac_nres(c) = area( ∪ NRES footprints ∩ c ) / area(c)
stored as frac_res_full / frac_nres_full in datasets/week4_pixel_gt/pixels.parquet
4

Join + mean → building soft GT

Week 6 joins inside cells to week4 GT on lon/lat, then averages. Same cells feed AE OOF means.

soft_gt_frac_res(B) = mean_{c ∈ inside(B)} frac_res_full(c)
soft_gt_frac_nres(B) = mean_{c ∈ inside(B)} frac_nres_full(c)
5

Score soft vs soft on balanced buildings

Per-metro 1:1 RES/NRES building balance (seed 42), then MAE on shared AE∩GHSL coverage — soft GT and hard GT both reported.
